## Relevant CEFR outcomes (summary)

### Pre-A1
- Recognise familiar words and very basic phrases (greetings, numbers, classroom language).
- Interact with help: introduce self, ask/answer simple personal questions.
- Begin to notice sound–spelling links and Dutch-specific phonemes.

### A1
- Understand familiar everyday expressions and very short texts (signs, forms, menus).
- Use present tense for routines, needs, shopping, directions.
- Manage simple social exchanges with slow, clear speech.

### A2
- Understand phrases on familiar topics (work, family, travel, health).
- Describe background, immediate environment, and matters of immediate need.
- Use past time (perfect) for experiences; make appointments and simple phone/email messages.

### B1
- Understand main points of clear standard input on familiar matters (work, school, leisure).
- Deal with most travel situations; produce simple connected text on familiar topics.
- Describe experiences, events, dreams, hopes; briefly give reasons and opinions.

## Learning progression on this site

1. **Pre-A1 (modules 1–4):** alphabet, pronunciation, survival greetings, numbers/time/classroom language.
2. **A1 (modules 5–12):** personal world — home, food, shopping, town, work/hobbies/weather/health — with present tense and de/het.
3. **A2 (modules 13–18):** plans, travel, perfect tense, housing/health, social/work/restaurant, feelings and messages.
4. **B1 (modules 19–24):** narrative and opinion, workplace and media texts, formal admin, society and goals.

Each module: 3 short lessons + checkpoint; minimum 15 exercises including varied skills.

## Module–CEFR skill mapping (overview)

| Level | Primary skills emphasised | Grammar spine |
|-------|---------------------------|---------------|
| Pre-A1 | pronunciation, listening, vocabulary | pronouns, capitalisation, time phrases |
| A1 | vocabulary, speaking, reading | present tense, niet/geen, questions, de/het |
| A2 | grammar, writing, listening | perfectum, separable verbs, future with *gaan* |
| B1 | reading, writing, speaking | subordinate clauses, *zou*, formal register |

## Teaching assumptions

- **Target variety:** Netherlands Dutch (standard Northern pronunciation focus).
- **Audience:** adult/young adult independent learners; English used as support language in UI prompts where helpful.
- **Communicative goal:** can-do tasks over table-only grammar; grammar is embedded in topics.
- **Spelling:** contemporary Dutch orthography; learners type answers (exercise normalisation handled in app layer).
- **Not exam training:** pacing and tasks are inspired by CEFR/ERK but not tied to one exam board.
